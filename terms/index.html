The agreement

The service

Meezaan helps you keep track of your daily worship: the Farḍ Salah and their Sunnah, Witr, Dhikr, Quran and Sadaqa. It calculates prayer times on your device, shows the Qibla direction, and keeps a record of what you have marked.

It is offered free of charge and without advertising by the Feedingo Foundation as a Sadaqah Jariyah. There is nothing to buy inside it, no subscription, and no payment is ever taken.

Not a religious ruling

Points, levels, streaks and time windows are encouragement, not rulings of Islamic law. A Meezaan score is for personal motivation only. It does not represent your actual reward with Allah, since the acceptance of every deed depends upon sincerity, which only Allah knows.

Prayer times are calculated on your phone and may differ from your local masjid by a few minutes. When in doubt, follow your masjid and ask a qualified scholar.

The Quran text, translations and recitation shown in the app come from AlQuran Cloud and the Islamic Network. We present them as received and do not alter them.

Children
