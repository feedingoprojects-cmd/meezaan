In short

Meezaan is a worship tracker. We keep the little we need to make it work, and nothing more. This page says exactly what that is, in the order it matters.

We do not sell your data. We do not show advertising. There is no advertising identifier, no ad network and no profiling anywhere in the app.

Your record of worship belongs to you. You can erase all of it from inside the app, in one place, at any time.

Who we are

Meezaan is built and operated by the Feedingo Foundation, a youth movement based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India. The Foundation is the controller of the data described here. Our address and contact details are at the end of this page.

What we collect

Everything below is collected because a part of the app would not work without it. There is no other collection.

Your account

WhatWhy we hold it
NameTo address you in the app and to show you to your friends and on the community board, if you take part.
Email addressTo sign you in, to recover the account, and to send account email such as confirming the address or resetting the password. We do not send marketing.
PasswordHeld by Google Firebase Authentication, not by us. We never see it.
A photo, if you sign in with GoogleGoogle passes us your name, email address and profile photo when you choose that way of signing in. The photo is shown only to you.
Date of birth and gender, if you give themDate of birth is private and is used only to tell whether an account belongs to a child, so that the app can behave accordingly. Gender selects the voice used by the guided tour. Both may be left out.
Phone number, if you give itAccount recovery only. It is private, it is never shown to another user, and it is never used for marketing or passed to anyone.
A public ID, for example ST-K7P2M4Generated by us. It is how a friend adds you without needing your email address or phone number.

Your worship record

WhatWhy we hold it
Which deeds you marked, and on which dayThis is the app. It is what the home screen, the calendar and the statistics are made of.
The points, target and tasbih points of each dayStored with the day so that a past day keeps the score it was marked with, even if the points are later adjusted.
Your level, and the date it was chosenTo know what a complete day means for you, and to hold a level for its week before it can be changed again.
Streaks, perfect days, total points and unlocked achievementsTo show your progress and your rewards, and to place you on the community board if you take part in it.
The date you started trackingSo that the record begins on the day you joined and days before it are not counted as missed.

Your settings and place

WhatWhy we hold it
One approximate location: a single pair of coordinates, your city, country and timezonePrayer times and the Qibla direction are calculated from it, on your phone. It is saved with your profile so your times are right when you sign in on another device. It is a coarse point, it is overwritten when it changes, no history of your movements is kept, and no other user ever sees it. You can type your city by hand instead of using the phone's location.
Language, calculation method, madhab, high latitude rule, clock formatSo the app looks and calculates the same way on every device you sign in on.
Whether you appear on the community board, whether reminders are on, whether late marking is allowedYour choices, applied across your devices.

Technical records

WhatWhy we hold it
Sign in records: the time, the platform (Android or web) and the app version, with your name, public ID and, where given, phone number and ageTo support you when something goes wrong, to notice misuse of an account, and for the Foundation's own records. Only recent entries are kept.
Presence: when you were last active, the app version and which screen is openSo that the people running the app can see that the service is healthy and help when someone reports a problem. It is not a history: only the latest moment is stored.
A one way code made from an email address, kept for one daySo that nobody can use the password reset form to flood a stranger's inbox. The address itself is not stored by this.
Crash and error reportsSee error reports below.

On your phone only

Some things never leave the device: today's tasbih count, the cached copy of your day so the app opens instantly and works offline, your chosen language, whether you have seen the guided tour, and the app's own configuration cache. Signing out or deleting the account clears them.

What we never collect

  • No advertising identifier, no ad network, no advertising profile. There is no advertising in Meezaan.
  • No analytics SDK that follows you around, and no third party tracker.
  • No contacts, no call logs, no SMS, no files, no photo library.
  • No continuous or precise location tracking, and no location history.
  • No payment details. The app is free and has nothing to buy.
  • No audio, no video, and no photograph is ever taken, stored or uploaded by the app.

Permissions the app asks for

Android will ask you for these. Each one is used for one thing only.

PermissionWhat it is for
Approximate locationPrayer times and the Qibla direction. Coarse, not precise. You can refuse it and set your city by hand.
NotificationsSalah reminders, and a quiet note when a friend sends a request. Scheduled on the phone.
Alarms and reminders, and display over other appsSo the reminder for a Farḍ prayer arrives at the minute it is due, and the full alarm screen can open over the lock screen when you choose that kind of reminder.
CameraOnly to read a friend's QR code. The picture is read in the moment and is never saved or sent anywhere. You can scan a photo of a code instead and never grant it.
Vibration, run at startup, ignore battery optimisationSo a reminder can vibrate, so reminders survive a restart, and so that strict battery managers do not silence them. The battery exemption is always your choice.
InternetTo sign in and to sync your record. The app still works offline and syncs when the signal returns.

Where it is kept, and who else handles it

Your data is held on our behalf by a small number of services. They process it only to provide the app, under their own agreements with us, and none of them is permitted to sell it.

ServiceWhat it handles
Google Firebase AuthenticationSigning in: your email address, your password, and Google sign in.
SupabaseThe database that holds your profile, settings, worship record, achievements and friendships.
SentryCrash and error reports, as described below.
AlQuran Cloud and the Islamic NetworkThe Quran text, translations and recitation. When you open the Quran reader, your device requests those directly, which means your IP address is visible to them. We send them nothing about you.
Google PlayDistribution of the app. Google's own policies apply to the store itself.

Your record is tied to your account: nobody can read it without signing in as you. Administrators are the exception, and that is described next.

Administrators

A small number of people at the Foundation run Meezaan. They can see account details, including name, email address, city, activity and sign in records, in order to help users, to answer questions, and to keep the community safe. They cannot read your password. Sign in records may be exported for the Foundation's own records. Administrator accounts are protected with two factor authentication.

What other people can see

On the community board and to your friends, other users see your name, your level, your points, your streak and your league. That is all.

They never see your email address, your phone number, your date of birth, your gender, your city or your location. You can leave the board at any time in Settings, Privacy, and your name disappears from it.

Children and families

Level 1 of Meezaan is made for children, and the app is suitable for them: there is no advertising, nothing to buy, no chat, no photographs and no open messaging of any kind. A friend connection shows only a name and a streak.

We expect a parent or guardian to create and look after a child's account, and we ask that children use the app with their permission. We do not knowingly collect anything from a child beyond what is listed on this page. If you believe a child has given us more than that, write to us and we will remove it.

How long we keep it

  • Your profile and your worship record stay for as long as your account exists.
  • Sign in records are kept short: only recent entries are stored.
  • Presence is a single moment, overwritten each time you open the app.
  • The one way code made from an email address is deleted after a day.
  • Crash reports are kept by Sentry for a limited period and then removed.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings, then Delete account. This removes your profile, your deed records, your points, your streaks, your achievements and your friendships. It cannot be undone.

If you would rather ask us to do it, write to projects@feedingo.org from the address on the account and we will erase it and confirm when it is done.

If an administrator pauses your account, the app also clears what it had saved on your phone, so a device that has been cut off does not keep a readable copy.

Error reports

When something goes wrong in the app, a technical report is sent to Sentry so we can fix it: what failed, on which screen, the app version and the kind of phone, together with your public ID so that one person's repeated problem can be recognised.

It never includes your name, your email address, your phone number, your location or your record of deeds.

Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • See what we hold: most of it is visible in the app, and we will send you the rest on request.
  • Correct anything that is wrong, from your profile screen.
  • Erase everything, from Settings, Delete account, or by writing to us.
  • Leave the community board without leaving the app.
  • Refuse any permission and still use the app, with the part that needs it switched off.

Write to projects@feedingo.org for any of these and we will answer within thirty days.

Security

Traffic between the app and our services is encrypted. Your record is protected at the database by row level rules, which means a signed in account can read its own rows and no others. Administrator accounts require a second factor. No service can promise perfect security, but we keep what we hold small on purpose, which is the best protection of all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears here with a new date at the top, and the same text appears inside the app under About, Privacy policy. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
